## 1. SCOPE OF WORK

The Contractor shall provide licensed electrical services for the hardwired interconnection of smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarm units at residential properties, as directed by LFP on a per-job basis.

Work includes:
- Hardwiring and interconnecting smoke and/or CO alarm units at residential properties
- Ensuring all electrical work meets the Ontario Electrical Safety Code (OESC) and Ontario Building Code (OBC)
- Testing interconnection functionality upon completion

## 2. COMPENSATION

- **Base rate:** $125.00 per house (up to 3 units interconnected)
- **Additional units:** $25.00 per unit beyond 3
- **Payment terms:** Net 15 from date of invoice
- **Payment method:** E-transfer or cheque

The Contractor shall submit an invoice to LFP after each completed job or on a weekly summary basis, as mutually agreed.

## 3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee of LFP. The Contractor:

- Controls their own methods, tools, and schedule for completing the work
- Supplies their own tools, equipment, and transportation
- May work for other clients and businesses
- Is responsible for their own income tax remittances, CPP contributions, and any other statutory deductions
- Is not entitled to employee benefits, vacation pay, or any other employment entitlements
- May decline individual jobs without penalty

Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture.

## 4. INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

The Contractor shall maintain, at their own expense, the following insurance coverage throughout the term of this Agreement:

- **Commercial General Liability (CGL):** Minimum $2,000,000 per occurrence
- **Certificate of Insurance:** Must name London Fire Protection Inc. as an additional insured
- The Contractor shall provide a copy of their certificate of insurance to LFP prior to commencing any work, and upon each annual renewal

## 5. LICENSING

The Contractor warrants that they hold a valid Electrical Safety Authority (ESA) licence and will maintain it in good standing throughout the term of this Agreement. The Contractor shall provide a copy of their current ESA licence to LFP prior to commencing any work.

## 6. WSIB (WORKPLACE SAFETY AND INSURANCE)

Option A: The Contractor provides their own WSIB Clearance Certificate to LFP prior to commencing work and upon each renewal.

Option B: If the Contractor does not carry their own WSIB coverage, LFP will report the Contractor's earnings under LFP's WSIB account as required by Ontario law.

*(Circle or initial the applicable option)*

The Contractor shall provide a current WSIB Clearance Certificate to LFP prior to commencing any work (if Option A applies).

## 7. NON-SOLICITATION

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following termination, the Contractor shall not directly solicit or provide fire protection services to any client of LFP that the Contractor was introduced to through work performed under this Agreement.

## 8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing fourteen (14) days' written notice to the other party. LFP may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice in the event of:

- Safety violations or unsafe work practices
- Loss of ESA licence
- Lapse in required insurance coverage
- Breach of confidentiality or non-solicitation provisions

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

The Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless London Fire Protection Inc., its owners, officers, and employees from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including legal fees) arising from or related to the Contractor's electrical work, including but not limited to:

- Property damage caused by the Contractor's work
- Personal injury arising from the Contractor's work
- Any breach of the Ontario Electrical Safety Code

## 10. WARRANTY OF WORK

The Contractor warrants that all work performed under this Agreement shall:

- Meet the requirements of the Ontario Electrical Safety Code (OESC)
- Meet the requirements of the Ontario Building Code (OBC)
- Be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner
- Pass any subsequent ESA inspection if required

The Contractor shall correct any defective work at no additional cost to LFP within a reasonable timeframe.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Contractor shall keep confidential all client information, pricing, business processes, and proprietary information of LFP encountered during the performance of work under this Agreement.

## LEGAL NOTES (NOT LEGAL ADVICE)

*The following are practical recommendations — not legal advice. Consult a lawyer for legal guidance specific to your situation.*

- **CRA Independent Contractor criteria:** This arrangement satisfies the key CRA indicators — the Contractor controls their own tools, methods, and schedule; can profit or lose on work; and is free to work for others.
- **Before first job:** Obtain and verify the Contractor's ESA licence, certificate of insurance (with LFP as additional insured), and WSIB clearance certificate.
- **Insurance:** Check certificate of insurance annually at renewal.
- **WSIB:** If the Contractor does not have their own WSIB coverage, LFP must report the Contractor's earnings under LFP's WSIB registration.
- **ESA permit:** Confirm whether individual jobs require an ESA permit/notification — this depends on the scope of electrical work and local ESA requirements.
