Privacy Policy

London Fire Protection Corp ("we", "us", or "our") operates internal business tools to manage customer communications and bookkeeping. This policy describes how we handle data.

Information We Collect

We collect information submitted through our website contact form, including name, email address, phone number, and service inquiry details. We also collect business financial data within our internal accounting tools.

How We Use Information

Customer information is used solely to respond to service inquiries and manage our business operations. Financial data is used for internal bookkeeping and tax compliance purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties except as required by law or to deliver services you have requested (e.g., email delivery providers).

Third-Party Services

Our internal tools integrate with QuickBooks Online (Intuit Inc.) for accounting purposes. Data shared with QuickBooks is governed by Intuit's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at info@londonfireprotection.ca.
